Architecture · modules

Architecture

Both adapters publish every operation of the composed host, derived from the modules' descriptors. packages/host composes the modules in dependency order; core's write envelope runs every operation; source systems stay read-only.

ER · financial core

Financial core

Four records: expense ≠ payment, revenue ≠ receipt. Items are negative exactly in DEDUCTION financial categories; settlements match item signs and never exceed their item, which states its amount (ADR 7). Receipts and payments are nonnegative signed sums.

ER · sales and cash

Sales and cash

Every import stages its file as movements and posts by descriptor (ADR 16). Sale movements become the day's lines; revenue movements become revenues; receipt movements become receipts that settle them (ADRs 20 and 21).

Waterfall · result

Daily closing

Illustrative example: result = sold − cost of goods − fixed cost share. An open month keeps the day provisional.

Database schema · expenses

Physical schema — expenses

Composite Book/currency foreign keys enforce isolation. Settlements match item signs and cannot exceed their magnitude. Payment amounts are derived and ≥ 0; discounts are negative expenses in the same payment.

Database schema · authentication

Physical schema — authentication and tenancy

An agent credential belongs to a Book and its delegated operator must be a member. Composite keys enforce isolation across Books.
